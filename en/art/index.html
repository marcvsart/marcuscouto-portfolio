Where research becomes work: painting, cryptoart, literature and sound.

My artistic practice investigates the fantasies culture projects onto machines. In Portraits of the Basilisk, I ask an obsolete generative model, running locally and unfiltered, to show what it looks like, and I paint by hand what it returns: the singularity as eschatology with an engineering vocabulary. In sound, Osasco Dynamics imagines a dystopian tech company in full decline. In cryptoart, I use the blockchain as language and as archive, a permanent and dubious memory. These are cartographies of the technocracy: image, sound and text as strata of the same territory.

I’ve been producing electronic music since 2009, across electro, house and techno. As Missiles at a Wedding, I released EPs on Step In Recordings and TRNQR and appeared on the Hy Brazil Vol. 4 compilation, featured by Spin. With Victor Lucindo, I created Osasco Dynamics, a dystopian, satirical corporation in record form, reviewed by The Wire. The latest singles, Pouco Grave and Sem Sinal (feat. DJ Desisto), pave the way for an EP.
