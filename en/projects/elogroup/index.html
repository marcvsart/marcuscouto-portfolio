EloGroup

Creative direction and AI at a technology consultancy · Jul 2021 – Sep 2025

At EloGroup, a technology and AI consultancy, I came in through the written word, as editor of Insights, the firm’s thought-leadership publication. Writing pulled everything else along, and soon the company’s entire communication ran across my desk.

I built its creative direction from the ground up (website, social media art direction, visual identity, manifestos, the voice of the brand) and gave market shape to its business solutions and AI offerings, working directly with the CEO, partners and directors. I directed designers and writers.

It is also where generative AI entered the company’s communication: AI-made images in place of stock photography, which became the house standard, automated copywriting, AI-generated podcasts. Stock imagery left the stage, and every idea got an image made for it.
