Videocamp

Videocamp, Instituto Alana · Feb 2018 – Aug 2019

At Videocamp, part of Instituto Alana, I wrote the launch film and campaign for the Videocamp Film Fund: a US$400,000 fund unveiled at the United Nations alongside UNICEF, which drew more than 150 entries. Then I took over the platform’s communication. I wrote its tone of voice guide, made its pieces and drew up a social media strategy that brought in more people than the site could hold: it had to expand its server capacity to keep up.
