Prensa Autômata

A daily newspaper produced by AI · prensaautomata.com

A newspaper that writes and publishes itself. Every day, a system gathers, selects, writes and lays out its editions: the morning edition at 6 a.m., the evening edition Monday to Saturday at 6 p.m., and a special Sunday edition. Brazil, the world, science, AI, a Brazilian album, a poem, the regional weather forecast. The editions go to the website and the newsletter, with an animation on Instagram and X.

My part is direction: what goes in, in what order, in what tone and what stays out. The system executes; the editorial line is my design.
