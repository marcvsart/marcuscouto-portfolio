Journalism and media

Newsrooms and studios · 2008 – 2020

Before creative direction, there was the newsroom. Twelve years writing for people who read fast and in great numbers: that is where the instinct comes from. From the most recent layer down to the deepest:

Between 2016 and 2020, working freelance, I wrote for the Red Bull Music Academy Festival in 2017 and 2018 and for Sesc Jazz 2019, ran insight research for AKQA on the launch of Beck’s in Brazil, and made infographics for GOL’s inflight magazine.

At The Factory, from November 2016 to February 2018, I ran branded content projects for Natura, Abbott, AES Eletropaulo and AES Tietê from start to finish.

At Estúdio ABC, Editora Abril’s branded content studio, in 2015 and 2016, I wrote technology projects for IBM, Cisco and Renault.

At INFO, also at Abril, from July 2012 to August 2015, I was a reporter for the magazine and then editor of its website, leading a team. I covered technology from Bitcoin to drones and was nominated for the Prêmio Abril de Jornalismo in 2015.

At UOL, from October 2011 to July 2012, I was assistant editor for culture and entertainment. It was my first team.

At G1, Globo’s news site, from October 2010 to October 2011, I was a culture reporter: I interviewed Michael Bay in Los Angeles and covered Rock in Rio, Virada Cultural and FLIP.

At Época São Paulo, from June 2009 to October 2010, I came over from Rraurl to write about music and culture, with a nightlife column and film criticism.

At Link, Estadão’s technology section, from January 2008 to January 2009, I started with technology and culture, and with an interview with Kanye West.
