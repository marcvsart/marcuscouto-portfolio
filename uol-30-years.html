About
I was responsible for the visual execution of the concept for UOL's 30th-anniversary film — one of Brazil's largest media portals. The piece retells some of the company's defining milestones: historic broadcasts and pioneering partnerships with artists and e-commerce companies. Every shot was built with generative AI models. The film became the centerpiece of the 30-year campaign and was screened to open a special session at the Brazilian Senate, during a tribute honoring UOL.
